About Me

I'm a passionate software engineering student and engineer at Liquid Intelligent Technologies and Blossom Tech Solutions, where I blend my expertise in mobile development, backend systems, and network engineering to create innovative solutions that bridge technology and telecommunications.

My journey in technology spans multiple domains: from building sophisticated Android applications using Java and Kotlin, to architecting robust backend systems with Python, Rust (Axum), and Flask, and optimizing telecom networks with cutting-edge routing technologies.

I specialize in creating fraud prevention systems, IoT solutions, and telecom optimization platforms. My work includes developing MM Assist (a Mobile Money Fraud Prevention app), custom video players, USSD automation systems, and pioneering research in telecom security through the TOSS (Telecom Optimization & Security System) project.

Beyond coding, I'm deeply involved in network infrastructure, working with Mikrotik, TP-Link, and Cambium routers, implementing RADIUS authentication, hotspot systems, and cloud-based solutions on AWS. I also explore AI/ML applications, building predictive analytics bots for sports and data-driven insights.
