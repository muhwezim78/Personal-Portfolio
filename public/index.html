About Me

I'm a telecommunications engineer based in Kampala. I spend my days provisioning fibre subscribers at Savanna Fibre, and the rest of my time building and running TerraConnect Uganda — the billing and authentication platform seven hotspot businesses depend on to get paid.

Three years of field engineering came first: installing, aligning and troubleshooting Cambium ePMP and PTP 550 microwave links on Liquid Intelligent Technologies' network as a Japotech contractor. Thirty client links commissioned in 2025, nineteen fault escalations resolved. That work is why the software I write actually fits how a small ISP operates.

On the software side I work in Rust and Node.js on PostgreSQL, deployed as Docker services on Ubuntu, with FreeRADIUS wired to MikroTik hotspot routers over WireGuard tunnels for centralised authentication and accounting. Payments run through pawaPay for MTN MoMo and Airtel Money — which meant passing a multi-round provider due diligence covering AML/CFT policy, operator agreements and audit trails.

I also build client-facing software: a router management and billing system plus a Flutter app for QuickNet Networks in Nigeria, and a native Android music player published to Google Play.
